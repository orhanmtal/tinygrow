Privacy Policy

At TinyGrow, the security of your children's data is our utmost priority.

1. Data Collection

The TinyGrow app does not send any of the height and weight measurements you enter to our servers. There is no membership or account system within the application.

2. Data Storage

All measurement data is stored exclusively in your device's local storage. When you delete the app, this data is automatically deleted as well.

3. Third-Party Access

Your data is never sold or shared with any third-party organizations. TinyGrow is an ad-free application and does not track your data.
